Custom Shaped Rigid Gift Boxes

Direct answer for buyers

A custom shaped rigid gift box is engineered around a non-standard silhouette, product fit and opening method. ShineleeBox can start from your product, sketch, reference or AI concept, develop a manufacturable structure and produce a physical sample. MOQ starts from 50 pieces for eligible projects.

Shape plus opening mechanics

A silhouette alone is not enough. The lid clearance, hinge, drawer direction, support walls and assembly sequence must work together within production tolerances.

Insert engineered with the box

Paper, PET blister, EPE and EVA inserts can be evaluated for the product weight, orientation, removal method and display requirements.

Made for many applications

Custom shapes can support chocolate, dates, perfume, cosmetics, jewelry, promotional gifts and holiday collections when the complete product set is defined.

Concept development

From your AI concept to a manufacturable physical sample

Send an AI-generated packaging concept, visual rendering, sketch or reference image. Our packaging team reviews the geometry, opening method, board construction, wrap seams, inserts, materials and finishes before developing a production-oriented solution.

AI images can contain impossible joints, floating parts or unrealistic materials. We preserve the visual direction while recommending practical changes, then create a physical sample so you can verify appearance, strength, fit and unboxing before mass production.

  2. 02Review structure, material and production feasibility
  3. 03Confirm dimensions, insert, artwork and finishes
  4. 04Build and evaluate the physical box sample

Custom shaped box FAQ

Can you turn an AI concept into a sample?

Yes. We review feasibility, develop a manufacturable construction and create a physical sample after the specification is confirmed.

Which custom shapes are possible?

Heart, star, arch, crescent, half-moon and other silhouettes are possible when the dimensions, opening method and wrapping construction are feasible.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ starts from 50 pieces for eligible projects. Complex structures can require more; CS-007 starts at 500 pieces.

How long do sampling and production take?

Free sampling normally takes 5–7 days. Standard production normally takes 20–25 days after sample and artwork approval.

Can you make a fitted insert?

Yes. Paper, PET blister, EPE or EVA inserts can be engineered around product dimensions and presentation requirements.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the product size and weight, AI concept or desired shape, opening direction, quantity, logo artwork, destination and required date.

Have a shape or AI concept in mind?

Send the concept and product specification for a structural feasibility review and sample plan.
