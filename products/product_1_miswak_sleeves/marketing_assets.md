#### **Overview**
Elevate the traditional Sunnah practice with our **Premium Breathable Paper Miswak Sleeves**. Designed specifically for the modern traveler, these sleeves solve the age-old problem of moldy Miswaks caused by airtight plastic storage. Our proprietary microporous paper technology ensures your Miswak stays dry, fresh, and hygienic, whether it's in your suit pocket or travel bag.

#### **Key Features & Advantages**
- **Advanced Breathability:** The semi-translucent paper features engineered micropores that allow moisture to escape while preventing dust from entering. No more soggy or moldy Miswaks.
- **Eco-Friendly & Biodegradable:** Made from 100% sustainably sourced paper. Our sleeves are fully compostable, aligning with eco-conscious values and reducing plastic waste.
- **Waterproof Inner Coating:** Despite being breathable, the inner lining is treated with a food-grade, thin waterproof layer to prevent the natural oils of the Miswak from staining your clothes or bags.
- **Premium Aesthetics:** The translucent, high-texture paper offers a luxury feel suitable for high-end retail, hotel amenities, or airline travel kits.
- **Custom Branding Available:** We offer full-color printing for wholesale orders. Perfect for brands, Islamic organizations, or corporate gifts.

1. **Health First:** Plastic traps moisture, leading to rapid bacterial growth and mold. Our paper sleeves keep the wood dry and the bristles fresh.
