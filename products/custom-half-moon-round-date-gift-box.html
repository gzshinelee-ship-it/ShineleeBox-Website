How to Source a Custom 1kg Date or Chocolate Box

Order the existing design or light customization online from 60 boxes. For a full OEM project, provide the actual assortment—not only the target 1kg weight—so the box and divider system can be engineered correctly.

1. Product data

Date or chocolate dimensions, piece count, wrapping method, total filled weight and packing orientation.

2. Layout

Target diameter, number of rings, divider count, lid opening direction and desired reveal.

3. Branding

Logo artwork, paper color, foil, embossing, lamination and approved Ramadan or Eid details.

4. Order path

Use online wholesale for the existing structure, or request a full custom quote for new dimensions, materials, dividers or construction.

1kg Half-Moon Date & Chocolate Box FAQ

How much product can this half-moon gift box hold?+

The referenced configuration is designed for approximately 1kg of premium dates, chocolates or a mixed assortment. Final capacity depends on piece size, wrapping and compartment layout.

What is the minimum online order?+

The ready-stock and light-customization minimum is 2 cartons, equal to 60 boxes. Orders must use whole cartons of 30 boxes. Full OEM changes to size, structure or insert are quoted separately and may require a higher MOQ.

Can the logo, color and compartment layout be customized?+

The online options support a foil logo and/or custom printed artwork while keeping the current size, structure and insert. Changes to the box construction or compartment layout require a full custom quote.

Can the box be used for both dates and chocolates?+

Yes. The existing radial compartments can hold dates, chocolates or a mixed assortment. Product fit should be checked against the item dimensions and wrapping method.

Can food directly contact the packaging?+

Food-grade paper can be specified where direct contact is required. The final material and packing system should be confirmed for the food type and destination market.

Where is this gift box manufactured?+

It is manufactured by ShineleeBox in Panyu, Guangzhou, China.

What information is needed for a full custom quotation?+

Provide piece dimensions, total filled weight, assortment count, quantity, logo artwork, destination and required delivery date.
