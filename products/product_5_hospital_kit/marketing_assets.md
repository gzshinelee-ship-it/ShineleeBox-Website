### Product Description (2000 Characters)
**Title: Elevating Patient Care with the Hospital Wudu Kit - A Seamless Blend of Faith and Clinical Safety**

**Introduction:**
In medical settings, respecting a patient's spiritual needs is as crucial as their physical recovery. For Muslim patients, performing Wudu (ablution) before prayer can be challenging after surgery or when bedbound. Traditional water-based Wudu often leads to wet bandages, slippery floors, and patient discomfort. Our Hospital Wudu Kit provides a professional, dry, and sterile alternative that ensures religious compliance while maintaining the highest standards of hospital hygiene.

**What’s Inside the Kit?**
1. **Premium Tayammum Pad:** Made from compressed natural earth/clay, specifically designed for dry ablution. It is sterile, dust-free, and easy to handle even for patients with limited mobility.
2. **Super-Absorbent, Lint-Free Paper Towels:** These extra-thick, medical-grade towels are designed to provide a clean, dry feel. They are highly absorbent and leave no residue, ensuring the patient stays comfortable and dry.
3. **Step-by-Step Guide Card:** A clear, visual instruction card that guides the patient through the Tayammum process, providing peace of mind and independence.
4. **Moisture-Proof Resealable Bag:** Each kit is individually packed in a high-quality, moisture-resistant bag, making it easy to store and distribute within a clinical environment.

**Why Choose Our Hospital Wudu Kit?**
- **Safety First:** Eliminates the risk of water spills on hospital floors, reducing the chance of slips and falls for both patients and staff.
- **Clinical Integrity:** Keeps surgical dressings and medical equipment dry, preventing infections associated with moisture.
- **Patient Dignity:** Empowers patients to fulfill their religious obligations independently, contributing to their emotional and psychological well-being during recovery.
- **Efficiency for Staff:** Reduces the time and effort required by nursing staff to assist with traditional water-based ablution.
- **Cost-Effective Procurement:** Available in bulk packaging (50 sets per box), designed for easy inventory management in hospitals, clinics, and nursing homes.

**Technical Specifications:**
- Material: Natural compressed earth, 100% Virgin wood pulp paper.
- Sterility: Non-sterile (Clean-room processed).
- Packaging: 1 Set/Individual Bag, 50 Bags/Carton.
- Compliance: ISO 9001, Halal Certified components.
