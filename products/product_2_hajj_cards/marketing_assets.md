### Product Description (Approx. 2000 Characters)
**Elevate Your Spiritual Journey with the Ultimate Hajj & Umrah Tracking Cards**

Focus on your devotion, not the numbers. During the crowded and emotionally intense rituals of Tawaf and Sa'i, it is easy to lose count of your laps. Our Premium Waterproof Hajj Tracking Cards provide a reliable, physical way to ensure your rituals are completed accurately without the distractions of a smartphone or the worry of battery life.

**Key Features:**

*   **Durable & Waterproof Synthetic Paper:** Made from high-quality synthetic material, these cards are 100% waterproof and tear-resistant. Whether it's sweat, splashes from Zamzam water, or humid weather, your tracking cards remain intact and legible throughout your entire journey.
*   **Physical 7-Lap Counter:** Specifically designed for the 7 laps of Tawaf and Sa'i. Use the included physical markers or simply your thumb to keep track. The visual system is intuitive and works even in the densest crowds.
*   **Dual-Sided Functionality:** 
    *   **Front Side:** A clear, easy-to-use counting system for tracking your progress.
    *   **Back Side:** Essential Duas and prayers for Hajj and Umrah, presented in clear Arabic calligraphy with accurate English translations and transliterations.
*   **Comfortable Adjustable Lanyard:** Keep your cards accessible at all times. The premium lanyard is soft on the skin and features an adjustable length, ensuring it fits comfortably over your Ihram or everyday clothing.
*   **Safe & Ergonomic Design:** Features rounded corners to prevent scratching or discomfort during movement. The compact size fits perfectly in your hand or against your chest.
*   **Perfect Gift for Pilgrims:** A thoughtful and practical gift for friends and family embarking on their spiritual journey. Ideal for Hajj, Umrah, or Ziarat.

**Why Choose Physical Over Digital?**
Using a phone in the Haram can be distracting, and screens are often hard to see in bright sunlight. Our tracking cards require no battery, have no glare, and allow you to stay fully present in your prayers and connection with the Almighty. 

**Specifications:**
- Material: Waterproof Synthetic Paper
- Dimensions: 85mm x 120mm (Standard Card Size)
- Includes: Set of Tracking Cards + 1 Premium Lanyard
- Language: English & Arabic

Don't let the distraction of counting pull you away from your spiritual focus. Trust our Hajj Tracking Cards to be your reliable companion in the Holy Cities.
