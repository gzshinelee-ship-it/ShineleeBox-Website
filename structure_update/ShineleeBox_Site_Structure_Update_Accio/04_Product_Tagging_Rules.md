# Product Tagging Rules for ShineleeBox

Each product should be assigned to three types of tags:

## 1. Product Category Tags
These describe what the product is structurally.

Examples:
- Advent Calendar Boxes
- Advent Calendar Gift Set
- Perfume Packaging
- Cosmetic Packaging
- Drawer Gift Boxes
- Magnetic Gift Boxes
- Custom Shape Boxes
- Interactive Packaging
- LED Light-Up Gift Boxes
- Music Box Packaging
- Video Box Packaging

## 2. Application Tags
These describe what the buyer can use it for.

Examples:
- Perfume & Fragrance Packaging
- Beauty & Skincare Packaging
- Chocolate & Food Packaging
- Jewelry & Accessories Packaging
- Corporate & Retail Packaging
- Religious & Cultural Gift Packaging
- Personal Care Packaging
- Electronics & Premium Gift Packaging
- Small Gift Packaging

## 3. Holiday & Occasion Tags
These describe the seasonal or event use.

Examples:
- Christmas Packaging
- Holiday Gift Campaign
- Limited Edition Gift Set
- Ramadan & Eid Packaging
- Valentine’s Day Packaging
- Wedding & Anniversary Packaging
- Baby & Family Keepsake Packaging
- Graduation Packaging
- Mother’s Day / Father’s Day Packaging
