Packaging Basics

What Is Keepsake Packaging?

Keepsake packaging is a durable presentation package intentionally designed to be retained and reused after the original product is removed. Its value comes from continued utility, emotional meaning or display quality—not simply from using thicker board. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

What Is Luxury Rigid Packaging?

Luxury rigid packaging uses a stiff paperboard structure wrapped with printed or specialty paper to create a durable presentation box. Unlike a folding carton, it normally arrives assembled and holds its shape. Luxury depends on fit, finish, opening experience and product protection—not board thickness alone.

Rigid Box vs Folding Carton

A rigid box uses thick non-collapsing board that is usually wrapped and delivered assembled; a folding carton uses thinner paperboard that is printed, die-cut and typically shipped flat. Rigid boxes emphasize presentation and strength, while folding cartons usually offer lower weight and packing efficiency.

What Is Unboxing Experience Packaging?

Unboxing experience packaging is packaging engineered to control how a recipient opens, discovers and removes a product. It combines structure, reveal sequence, tactile cues, messaging and product access; decoration alone does not create a good unboxing experience. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

What Makes Luxury Packaging Worth Keeping?

Luxury packaging becomes worth keeping when it offers lasting utility, emotional relevance or display value and remains durable enough for repeated handling. High cost, heavy board or decorative finishes alone do not make a package retainable. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

Magnetic Box vs Drawer Box

A magnetic box usually opens like a book with a hinged lid and concealed magnets; a drawer box uses an outer sleeve and sliding inner tray. Magnetic boxes create a broad reveal, while drawer boxes create a controlled pull-out reveal and can offer strong product containment.

How to Choose an Insert

Choose a packaging insert from the product’s geometry, filled weight, fragile surfaces, presentation position, removal method and shipping risk. Paperboard, molded pulp, PET, EPE and EVA provide different appearance, retention and end-of-life characteristics. Final selection requires a physical fit sample using the real product.

Interactive packaging is packaging that prompts or responds to user action beyond basic opening and product removal. Interaction can be mechanical, sensory, electronic or connected—for example drawers, layered reveals, light, sound, video or NFC-linked content. Video Box vs Traditional Gift Box

A video box integrates a screen, controls, speaker and power system into the packaging, while a traditional gift box relies on structure, print and physical reveal. Video boxes deliver richer communication but add cost, weight, testing, charging, transport and end-of-life responsibilities.

Advent Calendar

How to Design a Custom Advent Calendar

To design an Advent calendar box, freeze the complete product assortment, map each item to a numbered cavity, engineer product retention and removal, then approve the structure before final artwork. A packed prototype is essential because one incorrect cavity can affect the whole calendar.

24-Day vs 12-Day Advent Calendar

A 24-day Advent calendar provides a longer daily countdown and needs more products, cavities, artwork and packing operations. A 12-day calendar is usually more compact and can support larger products or a lower total project cost. The right format depends on campaign timing, assortment and target retail price.

Gourmet Packaging

Chocolate Box Size Guide

Chocolate box size must be calculated from the actual chocolate dimensions, piece count, arrangement, wrappers or cups, removal clearance and lid clearance. Net weight alone is not enough because shapes and decorations change the occupied volume. Confirm the final size with real chocolates in a physical sample.

Best Inserts for Chocolate

The best chocolate insert safely separates the pieces, supports presentation and uses an appropriate food-contact layer for the intended market. Food-grade paper cups, paperboard grids and formed trays suit different shapes and packing processes. Greyboard is structural and normally should not directly contact unwrapped chocolate.

How to Package Medjool Dates

Package Medjool dates from their actual size range, piece count, filled weight and moisture or oil-control needs. Large dates require more cavity volume than smaller varieties at the same net weight. Use a suitable food-contact cup, tray, liner or individual wrap and confirm capacity with a filled sample.
