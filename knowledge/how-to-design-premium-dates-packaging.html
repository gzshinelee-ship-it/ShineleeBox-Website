How to Design Premium Dates Packaging

How date variety, piece size, filled weight, compartments and food-contact materials determine a premium date box.

Direct Answer

Premium dates packaging should be designed from the date variety, individual dimensions, piece count, net weight and arrangement. Because equal weights can occupy different volumes, final capacity must be confirmed with a filled physical sample. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

Key Considerations

  • Do not size a 1kg date box from weight alone.
  • Radial or grid compartments can separate varieties and flavors.
  • Dates need a suitable food-contact layer or individual wrapping.
  • Seasonal artwork should follow buyer-approved cultural and language review.

Engineering Considerations

Capacity and assortment

Large Medjool dates and smaller varieties have different volumes. Measure realistic product samples and define whether the assortment is loose, cupped, wrapped or chocolate-coated.

Structure and compartments

Round, half-moon, drawer, lid-and-base and calendar formats can work. Compartment count should follow the assortment and provide adequate finger clearance.

Ramadan and Eid planning

Allow time for structural development, sampling, artwork approval, production and transport. Confirm Arabic text and cultural symbols with the brand or an appropriate reviewer.

Information required for engineering and quotation

  • Product dimensions, filled weight and presentation orientation
  • Order quantity and destination country
  • Preferred structure, materials and finishes
  • Logo artwork or an AI concept/reference image
  • Required delivery date and packing method

FAQ

How big should a 1kg date box be?

It depends on variety, piece dimensions, count, wrapping and layout; confirm with a filled sample.

Can dates touch greyboard?

Greyboard is normally structural. Specify an appropriate food-grade primary layer or wrap.

What is the MOQ for the CS-007 half-moon box?

The customized CS-007 configuration starts at 500 pieces.
