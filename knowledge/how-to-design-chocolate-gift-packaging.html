How to Design Chocolate Gift Packaging

A buyer guide to piece count, cavities, food-contact layers, presentation and transport for custom chocolate boxes.

Direct Answer

Design chocolate gift packaging from the exact chocolate dimensions, piece count, arrangement and wrapping method. The rigid presentation box and the food-contact tray or liner should be specified separately, then tested with the real chocolates and intended distribution conditions. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

Key Considerations

  • Chocolate dimensions can vary by flavor, decoration and coating.
  • Cavity clearance must protect finishes without allowing excess movement.
  • Greyboard is normally not the direct food-contact surface.
  • Temperature management is a logistics issue, not solved by decoration.

Engineering Considerations

Define the assortment

Provide dimensions and weight for every piece, including toppings and wrappers. Decide whether the customer should see the full assortment at once or discover layers.

Specify food-contact components

Use an appropriate food-grade tray, cup, paper, liner or individual wrap for the food and destination requirements. Confirm oil resistance, odor and migration needs with qualified suppliers.

Test presentation and delivery

Evaluate removal, scuffing, bloom risk from handling, lid clearance and movement. The shipper and thermal distribution plan should be reviewed separately.

Information required for engineering and quotation

  • Product dimensions, filled weight and presentation orientation
  • Order quantity and destination country
  • Preferred structure, materials and finishes
  • Logo artwork or an AI concept/reference image
  • Required delivery date and packing method

FAQ

Can chocolate touch printed paper directly?

Only when the complete food-contact material and printing system are suitable for the specific use and market requirements.

How are compartments sized?

From actual chocolate dimensions, manufacturing variation, wrapping and removal clearance.

Can one structure be reused seasonally?

Often, with new artwork or sleeves, provided the assortment and fit remain compatible.
