How Long Does Custom Packaging Production Take?

A realistic timeline covering briefing, engineering, sampling, approval, mass production and international transport.

Direct Answer

Standard custom packaging production normally takes about 20–25 days after the physical sample and final artwork are approved. Total project lead time is longer because it also includes briefing, engineering, 5–7 day sampling, revisions, packing and transport. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

Key Considerations

  • The production clock should start after approval, not at the first inquiry.
  • Complex inserts and special materials can extend the schedule.
  • Artwork changes after sampling can trigger new checks.
  • International transport must be planned separately.

Engineering Considerations

A complete project timeline

The sequence normally includes product measurement, structural proposal, quotation, sample, fit review, artwork, approval, production, inspection, packing and shipment. Each approval delay moves the final date.

Common causes of delay

Incomplete product dimensions, changing assortments, unavailable specialty paper, repeated color revisions and late compliance requests are common causes. Seasonal capacity can also affect timing.

How to reduce schedule risk

Freeze the product set early, provide accurate samples, approve a decision owner and set dates for structural and artwork sign-off. Allow contingency before launch.

Information required for engineering and quotation

  • Product dimensions, filled weight and presentation orientation
  • Order quantity and destination country
  • Preferred structure, materials and finishes
  • Logo artwork or an AI concept/reference image
  • Required delivery date and packing method

FAQ

How long is sampling?

A free custom sample normally takes 5–7 days after the requirements are confirmed.

How long is mass production?

Standard production normally takes 20–25 days after sample and artwork approval.

Is shipping included in that time?

No. Transport time depends on destination and method and must be added separately.
