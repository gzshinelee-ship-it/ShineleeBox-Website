Custom Packaging MOQ Explained

Why minimum order quantities vary and which structural, material and finishing choices affect the MOQ for custom boxes.

Direct Answer

A custom packaging MOQ is the smallest production quantity a supplier can make economically for a specific specification. It is affected by setup time, material minimums, tooling, printing, finishing, assembly and quality control—not just the number of finished boxes. Final specifications should be confirmed against the actual product, a physical sample and the intended packing and distribution method.

Key Considerations

  • A supplier-wide “MOQ from” figure is not a promise for every design.
  • Custom colors and specialty materials may carry their own minimums.
  • Complex assembly and inserts can change the viable quantity.
  • Prototype quantity and mass-production MOQ are different.

Engineering Considerations

Why MOQs exist

Printing plates, dies, machine setup, paper cutting and hand assembly require fixed preparation. Spreading those costs over very few boxes raises the unit cost and can make production impractical.

What can lower the MOQ

Using available papers, standard processes, fewer finishes and a proven structure may reduce minimums. Changing size to fit material sheets can also help, but it must not compromise product fit.

How to compare quotations

Compare the exact specification, not only quantity and unit price. Confirm tooling, sample, freight, packing, taxes and whether a repeat order will use the same setup.

Information required for engineering and quotation

  • Product dimensions, filled weight and presentation orientation
  • Order quantity and destination country
  • Preferred structure, materials and finishes
  • Logo artwork or an AI concept/reference image
  • Required delivery date and packing method

FAQ

What is ShineleeBox’s MOQ?

Eligible custom projects start from 50 pieces. Product-specific designs may require more; CS-007 starts at 500 pieces.

Is a lower MOQ always cheaper overall?

No. Small runs often have a higher unit cost because setup costs are spread across fewer boxes.

Can MOQ change after sampling?

Yes, if the approved structure, materials or finishing process differs from the initial brief.
