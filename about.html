About ShineleeBox

Packaging That Lives Beyond the Product

Guangzhou Shinelee Paper Product Co., Ltd., the company behind ShineleeBox, was officially established in 2011, building on more than 18 years of experience in paper packaging and premium gift box development.

Based in Guangzhou, China, we specialize in premium custom packaging, creative structural packaging and memorable unboxing experiences for beauty, fragrance, chocolate, gourmet, gifting, lifestyle and seasonal brands worldwide.

We believe great packaging should do more than protect a product. It should create anticipation before opening, deliver a memorable moment during unboxing, and remain useful or meaningful after the product is gone.

A Box They Keep Is A Brand They Remember.

Designed for a Second Life

The Product May Be Gone. The Packaging Stays.

We design packaging with its second life in mind. After the original product is removed, the package can continue as a jewelry box, beauty organizer, keepsake box, memory box, desktop storage piece or decorative object.

This can extend the useful life of the packaging and keep the brand present in the customer’s everyday environment. Actual retention and reuse depend on the design and user behavior.
