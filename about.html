Get to Know Us

Company Profile & Positioning

For over 18 years, Guangzhou Shinelee Paper Product Co., Ltd. has delivered exceptional unboxing experiences to global brands.

Our Core Philosophy

At ShineleeBox, we believe packaging is not just a container — it is the first physical interaction a customer has with your brand. Our design and production philosophy is captured in two short sentences:

"Packaging That Gets Shared, Not Discarded."
"Too Good To Throw Away."

We achieve this by engineering complex cardboard structures, choosing premium sensory papers, executing beautiful print finishings, and maintaining strict hand-assembly quality controls.

Guangzhou Direct Manufacturer

Our main manufacturing facility is located in Guangzhou, China. As a direct factory, we manage the entire value chain from custom structural dieline engineering, sampling, and mass printing, to hand assembling and final secure export cargo packing.
