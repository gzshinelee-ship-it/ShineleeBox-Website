Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Can you make custom packaging for cakes and cupcakes?

Yes. We can customize window cake boxes, cupcake boxes, pastry boxes, macaron boxes, cookie boxes, and bakery gift boxes with custom size, insert, window, logo, and finishing.

Q2: Can you make luxury chocolate boxes?

Yes. We manufacture rigid chocolate boxes, drawer chocolate boxes, tray boxes, round boxes, magnetic chocolate gift boxes, and custom-shaped chocolate packaging.

Q3: Can you make dates boxes for Ramadan and Eid?

Yes. We support custom dates gift boxes, Ramadan food boxes, Eid sweet boxes, arch window boxes, crescent boxes, drawer dates boxes, and premium halal gift packaging.

Q4: What is the MOQ?

MOQ starts from 50 pcs for selected custom packaging projects. Final MOQ depends on box structure, size, material, printing, and finishing.

Q5: Can you provide inserts for food packaging?

Yes. We can provide paperboard inserts, PET trays, blister trays, EVA inserts, cupcake holders, chocolate trays, and custom compartment inserts.

Q6: Can I customize the box with my logo?

Yes. We support logo printing, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, Pantone color matching, and full custom artwork.

Q7: Can you make food packaging with a clear window?

Yes. We can make front windows, top windows, arch windows, crescent windows, and custom die-cut windows using clear PET material.

Q8: Can you help design the box structure?

Yes. We can help create the structure based on your product size, weight, display requirements, shipping needs, and gifting experience.
