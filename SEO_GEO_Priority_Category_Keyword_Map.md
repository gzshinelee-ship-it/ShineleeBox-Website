## Keyword ownership and cannibalization rules

1. Use the **primary keyword only on its assigned category page** as the main Title/H1 target.
2. Product pages must target a specific structure + application, for example `custom round chocolate gift box with compartments`, not the broad category term `custom chocolate box manufacturer`.
3. Blog posts target questions and comparisons. They should link to the relevant category page using descriptive anchors, but should not reuse the category page's exact Title/H1.
4. Application pages own industry intent; structure pages own box-form intent. A round date box product may appear in both collections, but its canonical product URL must remain singular.
5. Avoid creating replacement landing pages such as `/custom-wine-boxes/` or `/custom-chocolate-dessert-gift-boxes/` while the current application pages serve the same intent.

## Shared B2B facts to include naturally

- MOQ: 50 pieces
- Sample: free; 5–7 days
- Production: 20–25 days
- Custom sizing: supported from the buyer's product dimensions
- Inserts: paper, PET blister, EPE foam and EVA
- Food contact: food-grade paper is available
- Estimated shipping: United States 25–30 days; Europe about 50 days; Middle East about 50 days
- Inquiry response: within 2 hours
