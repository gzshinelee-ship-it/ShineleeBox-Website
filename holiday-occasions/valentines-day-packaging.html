ShineleeBox manufactures custom Valentine gift boxes for chocolates, jewelry, perfume, beauty products and promotional gift sets. Structures matched to the gift

Choose heart-shaped, drawer, magnetic, lid-and-base or countdown structures according to the product assortment, opening sequence and campaign positioning.

Custom inserts for product fit

We can engineer paper, PET blister, EPE or EVA inserts around exact chocolate, bottle, jewelry, candle or cosmetic dimensions where appropriate.

Brand-specific romantic design

Red and pink are optional—not mandatory. Match your own brand palette using printed paper, specialty wraps, foil stamping, embossing, ribbons and sleeves.

Design service

Turn your AI Valentine concept into a physical sample

Send an AI-generated packaging concept, visual rendering or inspiration image. Our team evaluates its structure, opening method, product insert, dimensions, materials and finishes, then develops a manufacturable proposal and real box sample.

AI visuals sometimes contain impossible joints, unsupported shapes or finishes that do not exist as shown. We recommend practical engineering changes while preserving the creative direction, so you can test product fit and the unboxing experience before production.

Custom Valentine packaging FAQ

What is the MOQ?

MOQ starts from 50 pieces for eligible structures. The final quantity depends on box style, dimensions, insert, printing and finishing.

Can you turn an AI concept into a real sample?

Yes. Send the AI concept or rendering. We assess feasibility, develop a production-oriented structure and create a physical sample after confirming the specification.

What products can the boxes hold?

We can design packaging for chocolates, jewelry, perfume, cosmetics, candles, accessories and multi-product Valentine gift sets.

How long do sampling and production take?

Free custom sampling normally takes 5–7 days. Standard production normally takes 20–25 days after sample and artwork approval.

Can the insert fit exact product dimensions?

Yes. Provide each product’s dimensions, weight and display orientation so the box and insert can be engineered around the complete gift set.

How quickly will you reply?

Qualified inquiries receive a reply within 2 hours. Include quantity, destination and required delivery date for a useful first response.

Need a different size, insert or finish?

Send your product dimensions, AI concept, quantity and delivery date. We will review the structure and sampling plan.
