Custom Ramadan packaging FAQ

Quick answers for brands, distributors and gift suppliers planning custom Ramadan and Eid packaging.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ starts from 50 pieces for eligible structures. The CS-007 1kg half-moon date box has an MOQ of 500. Final MOQ depends on construction, dimensions, insert and finishes.

Can you turn an AI concept into a sample?

Yes. Send an AI concept, rendering or reference image. We assess manufacturing feasibility, develop the structure and materials, then produce a physical sample after confirming the specification.

Can date boxes use food-grade materials?

Yes. Food-grade paper, trays, cups, liners or wraps can be specified for the food and destination requirements. Greyboard is normally the structural outer layer.

How long do sampling and production take?

Free custom sampling normally takes 5–7 days after requirements are confirmed. Standard production normally takes 20–25 days after sample and artwork approval.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send product dimensions and weight, piece count, packing layout, order quantity, logo artwork, destination and required delivery date.

How quickly will you reply?

Qualified packaging inquiries receive a response within 2 hours. Complete project details help us provide a useful first reply.

Plan your Ramadan or Eid packaging

Send your product information, AI concept or existing artwork. We will review the structure, sample plan and production requirements.
