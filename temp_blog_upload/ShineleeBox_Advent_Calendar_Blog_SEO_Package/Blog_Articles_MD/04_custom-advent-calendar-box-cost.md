# How Much Does a Custom Advent Calendar Box Cost?

For brand owners, product managers and gift set buyers, an advent calendar is not just a seasonal box. A well-designed custom advent calendar box can turn multiple small products into a premium limited-edition gift set, create daily unboxing moments, and make your packaging easier to share on social media.

At ShineleeBox, we focus on custom rigid paper packaging for B2B projects. This guide explains how to plan custom advent calendar box cost from a buyer’s point of view: structure, inserts, materials, finishing, MOQ, sampling and production details.

## Main factors that affect price

The cost of a custom advent calendar box depends on size, structure, number of drawers, material thickness, printing coverage, finishing, inserts, assembly complexity and quantity.

## Why structure changes cost

A simple drawer box costs less than a complex house-shaped, rotating or interactive advent calendar. More compartments mean more die-cutting, more assembly time and more QC steps.

## How inserts affect the price

Paperboard inserts, EVA inserts, pulp trays and plastic trays have different costs. Perfume and skincare products often need stronger inserts; chocolates and dates may need food-friendly tray solutions.

## Finishing options and budget

Foil stamping, embossing, debossing, spot UV, glitter paper, textured paper, ribbons, magnets, LED and music modules all improve presentation, but they also increase cost. Choose the finish that supports your brand value.

## MOQ and unit cost

Low MOQ is useful for testing, but unit cost is normally higher. Wholesale advent calendar box orders usually achieve better unit pricing because printing, tooling and setup costs are shared across more units.

## How to get an accurate quotation

To quote accurately, provide product dimensions, quantity, structure reference, artwork requirements, insert needs, target deadline and delivery country. Without these details, suppliers can only give a rough estimate.
