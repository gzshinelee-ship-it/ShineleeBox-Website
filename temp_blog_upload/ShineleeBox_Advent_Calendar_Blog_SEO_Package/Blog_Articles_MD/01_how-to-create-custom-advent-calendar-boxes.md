# How to Create Custom Advent Calendar Boxes for Your Brand

For brand owners, product managers and gift set buyers, an advent calendar is not just a seasonal box. A well-designed custom advent calendar box can turn multiple small products into a premium limited-edition gift set, create daily unboxing moments, and make your packaging easier to share on social media.

At ShineleeBox, we focus on custom rigid paper packaging for B2B projects. This guide explains how to plan custom advent calendar boxes from a buyer’s point of view: structure, inserts, materials, finishing, MOQ, sampling and production details.

## Why brands use custom advent calendar boxes

Brands use advent calendar boxes to introduce multiple products in one campaign, increase perceived value, and turn packaging into a marketing tool. Beauty, perfume, skincare, chocolate, jewelry and lifestyle brands can use the format for Christmas, holiday campaigns, product launches, PR gift sets and seasonal promotions.

## What products can be packed inside?

Common products include perfume samples, skincare minis, lipsticks, nail polish, candles, chocolates, jewelry, tea, dates, sweets and small accessories. The key is to confirm the size, weight and fragility of every product before the box structure and insert are finalized.

## Popular advent calendar box structures

Popular options include drawer advent calendar boxes, book style calendar boxes, double-door structures, round rotating boxes, house-shaped boxes, perfume-bottle-shaped boxes, carousel style boxes, LED light-up boxes and music boxes. Each structure creates a different unboxing experience and affects cost, shipping volume and assembly work.

## Custom options to confirm before sampling

Before sampling, confirm drawer quantity, overall size, rigid board thickness, insert material, artwork position, foil stamping, embossing, ribbon, magnetic closure, LED module or music module. This prevents repeated changes after the first sample.

## MOQ, sampling and production timeline

For selected custom projects, ShineleeBox can support low MOQ from 50 pcs, which is useful for premium trial campaigns, PR sets and small-batch brand launches. For larger wholesale orders, unit cost improves when the quantity increases. A good project starts with structural confirmation, then sample making, artwork approval, mass production and final QC.
