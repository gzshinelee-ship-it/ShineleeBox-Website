# Custom Interactive Packaging

## Packaging that gets noticed, opened, shared and remembered.

Interactive packaging adds sound, light or video to the unboxing experience. It helps brands turn ordinary product packaging into a campaign moment, a display piece and a stronger emotional connection with customers.

ShineleeBox develops custom interactive gift boxes for B2B buyers, including LED light-up boxes, music boxes, sound boxes, video boxes and custom advent calendar gift sets.

---

# Interactive Packaging Types

## LED Light-Up Gift Boxes
Custom gift boxes with LED lighting for perfume, beauty, chocolate, food gifts, PR kits and retail display packaging.

## Music Boxes
Packaging with built-in music modules for holiday gifts, beauty campaigns, keepsake boxes and promotional gift sets.

## Sound & Light Gift Boxes
Custom packaging that combines light and sound to create a stronger opening effect.

## Video Boxes
Gift boxes with LCD screens for brand storytelling, corporate gifts, wedding keepsakes and premium product presentations.

## Interactive Advent Calendar Boxes
Advent calendar boxes with lights, music or special opening effects for beauty, perfume, skincare, chocolate and holiday campaigns.
