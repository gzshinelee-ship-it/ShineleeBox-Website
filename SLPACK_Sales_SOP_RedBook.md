# SLPACK 全球销售话术红宝书

**定位：全球领先的“开箱仪式感”包装系统服务商**

---

## 1. 询盘回传 (The Hook - 建立第一心智)
**策略**：不报价格，先谈“系统”。让客户意识到他以前买的只是盒子，而我们要卖的是“流量”。

*   **话术示例**：
    > "Hi [Client Name], thank you for reaching out. At SLPACK, we don't just manufacture boxes; **we design 'Unboxing Experience Systems.'** Your project is more than packaging—it's your most powerful marketing tool for social media. Before we talk price, I’d like to understand the **'Dopamine Hit'** you want your customers to feel when they open each door..."

---

## 2. 品牌介绍 (The Authority - 亮出“信任状”)
**策略**：亮出 Disney 认证，建立“全球领先”专家形象。

*   **话术示例**：
    > "As a **Disney-certified supplier**, our engineering team specializes in the physics of surprise. We use **3mm High-Density MDF boards** and magnetic closures tested for 1,000+ clicks to ensure that every 'surprise' is consistent. This is why global high-end brands choose us to build their 'Experience Systems'..."

---

## 3. 细节确认 (The Consultation - 顾问式挖掘)
**策略**：问竞争对手不会问的专业问题（声音、结构、触感）。

*   **话术示例**：
    > "To build the perfect system, I need to confirm 3 'Experience Dimensions':
    > 1. **The Sound**: Crisp click vs soft hush?
    > 2. **The Tension**: Weighted luxury vs snap surprise?
    > 3. **The Light**: LED sensors to illuminate the core?"

---

## 4. 报价与谈判 (Handling Price - 价值锚定)
**策略**：客户讲价钱贵了，谈“单次开箱营销成本”和“社交媒体转化率”。

*   **话术示例**：
    > "I understand the price is higher than a standard paper box. But here is the logic: **A standard box has a 95% discard rate.** Our 'Royal Midnight' system has a **100% 'Instagrammable' rate.** If you save $2 on a cheap box, you might lose $2,000 in viral exposure. Do you want a container, or a **Dopamine-driven marketing system** that scales your brand automatically?"

---

## 5. 打样 (The Proof - 结构验证)
**策略**：强调我们要测试的是“体验”，而不只是“外观”。

*   **话术示例**：
    > "Let’s start with a **'Structural Prototype.'** We won't just test the print; we will test the 'Unboxing Sequence.' Once you feel the **'SLPACK Click,'** you’ll know exactly why your customers will be addicted to your brand."

---

## 👑 销售三大金律
1.  **禁用词**：尽量少用 'Box'，多用 'System' 或 'Experience'。
2.  **视觉锚点**：每一段话术必须配合 SLPACK 4.9 分高清渲染图。
3.  **价值转移**：把客户的预算从“采购部”转移到“市场部”。
